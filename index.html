Good to know before you order

Closed on Sundays

Every Chick-fil-A restaurant has been closed on Sundays since 1946.

Breakfast ends 10:30 AM

Breakfast is served Monday to Saturday, 6:30–10:30 AM.

Prices vary by location

Chick-fil-A publishes no national prices — the price at your restaurant can differ.

Pineapple Dragonfruit is permanent

The Pineapple Dragonfruit drinks joined the permanent menu in August 2026.

Save with the loyalty program

Members earn 10 points per $1. Silver (1,000 points) earns 11 per $1, Red (4,000) earns 12 per $1, and Signature (10,000) earns 13 per $1. Rewards start at 200 points, and tier points reset on 1 January.
